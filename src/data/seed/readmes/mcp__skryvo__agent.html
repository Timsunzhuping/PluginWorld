Skryvo for AI agents

Skryvo writes social posts and articles in your brand's voice, plans them on one calendar and publishes them to your channels. This repo connects it to the AI tool you already use: ask Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another agent to write a week of posts, check each one, and schedule it. Skryvo shows every post and waits for your go-ahead before anything is published.

You need a Skryvo account (free plan: https://app.skryvo.ai) and, to publish, accounts connected under Settings › Connected Accounts. Step-by-step setup for each agent: https://skryvo.ai/agents/

MCP server: https://api.skryvo.ai/mcp — one link for every user and client, not a secret. Clients that support OAuth open Skryvo's sign-in and consent page; no key needed. API key: for agents without OAuth, create one at https://app.skryvo.ai/settings/agents and keep it as the secret SKRYVO_API_KEY (or send it as Authorization: Bearer <key>).

What it can do

List brands and channels, write a post for up to four platforms (uses your plan's generations), schedule and cancel posts, list what is scheduled, and read results. Details: https://skryvo.ai/agents/

Where it publishes

Live: LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pinterest, Telegram, Discord, Slack, WordPress, WordPress.com and Dev.to. Built and waiting for platform review: LinkedIn Pages, Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads and TikTok (photo posts). What each channel supports and its limits: https://skryvo.ai/channels/

Channel guides for agents: LinkedIn MCP, X MCP, WordPress MCP, Discord MCP, Telegram MCP.

Plans

Free, Hobby, Pro and Enterprise; scheduling starts on Hobby. Limits and prices: https://skryvo.ai/pricing/

Privacy

Skryvo receives only the requests your agent makes, never the rest of your conversation. Full policy: https://skryvo.ai/privacy/. Disconnect apps or revoke keys any time in Skryvo under Settings › API & agents.
