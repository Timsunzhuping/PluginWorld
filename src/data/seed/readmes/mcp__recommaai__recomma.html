Recomma

Open-source tools from Recomma, which tracks what ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode say about your brand, and which sources they cite.

What is in here

What it does
packages/recomma npx recomma: a CLI and a typed TypeScript SDK for the Recomma API. Read visibility, share of voice and cited sources from a terminal or a script, and connect the MCP server to Claude, Codex, Cursor or VS Code in one command.
extension AI Answer Sources, a Chrome extension. Open it on a ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Mode answer and it lists every source the answer links to, grouped by domain, with your own site highlighted.

Why the sources an answer shows matter

An AI answer is assembled from the pages the engine retrieves, and the pages it cites are the ones a reader can click. In our study of 100 buying questions, each asked in ChatGPT's interface and through its API with search, the cited pages overlapped only 8% of the time, and the interface cited 7.8 domains per answer to the API's 3.2. A tool that only calls the API misses most of what people are actually shown.

The extension shows you one answer as a reader sees it. Recomma does the same thing for every question in your category, on every engine, every day, with the sample size beside every figure.

Quick start

# Connect Recomma to your AI assistant (Claude Code shown)
npx recomma mcp claude

# Or read your figures directly (REST API, Scale plan)
export RECOMMA_API_KEY=recomma_…
npx recomma brands
npx recomma visibility <brandId> --days 30
npx recomma sources <brandId> --kind competitor

Make a key under Settings → Connect AI at recomma.ai.
