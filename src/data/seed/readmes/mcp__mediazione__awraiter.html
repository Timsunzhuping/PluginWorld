AwrAIter — run your Telegram channels from one panel (or straight from ChatGPT / Claude)

awraiter.ai · Open the panel · Pricing · Support

AwrAIter is a panel for people who run Telegram channels: it builds a weekly content plan, drafts and schedules posts, publishes them to the channels you administer, and collects the statistics of everything published — subscriber growth, reach, ER/ERR, per-post performance, channel-vs-channel comparison.

It is also an MCP server. That means you can plug your Telegram channels directly into ChatGPT, Claude, or any MCP-capable AI assistant and manage them from the chat: "show last week's stats", "draft tomorrow's post from fresh sources and schedule it for 19:00". The assistant acts through your own account, with your own permissions.

What you get

Area What it does
Content plans A weekly plan per channel: topics, slots, rubrics. AI fills slots with drafts based on your channel's tone and sources.
Publishing Schedule or publish instantly to any channel where you've added our bot as admin. Text, photos, videos, albums, polls.
Sources & news Bind websites/RSS to a channel — fresh articles are scraped, deduplicated, and offered as draft material.
Analytics Subscribers, reach, ER and ERR, per-post views/reactions/forwards, comparison of channels over any period. Export to XLSX or auto-sync to Google Sheets.
Insights Every published post is scored against your channel's own 30-day median. With enough data the panel shows what statistically works: best day, best posting window, optimal length, whether ending with a question lifts engagement.
Teams & roles Invite teammates, restrict members to specific channels, manage roles.
MCP connector Included on every plan, free tier included. See MCP.md.

Quick start

  1. Sign in at awraiter.ai/login with the official Telegram login widget — no separate password exists.
  2. Connect a channel: add our service admin to your channel (the panel walks you through it), press Refresh state — the channel turns green when it's ready to publish.
  3. Add sources (optional): bind the websites your channel writes about; fresh articles start flowing in within the hour.
  4. Create a content plan: open Content plan, pick the channel, choose rubrics and posting frequency — the AI drafts the week.
  5. Review and publish: edit drafts, approve, and let the scheduler do the rest. Statistics appear in Analytics as posts go live.

One channel is free forever. Paid plans (from €9/month) add more channels, seats, sources and daily news volume — see pricing.

Connect it to your AI assistant

The 30-second version:

Connector URL:  https://awraiter.ai/mcp
Auth:           OAuth (sign in with Telegram) — or a personal access token from the panel

Paste that URL into Claude → Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, or ChatGPT → Settings → Connectors, authorize, and ask:

"List my channels and show this month's subscriber growth for the biggest one."
