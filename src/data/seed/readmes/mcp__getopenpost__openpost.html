What you can do

  • Connect accounts. Manage your social connections in one workspace. Each provider keeps its own capabilities and publishing limits.
  • Compose for each platform. Write posts and threads, then adjust the text and media for each account. Preview each version before publishing. Save account groups as Social Sets to reuse them.
  • Image editor. Design images and multi-page carousels with layers, templates, and custom fonts. Remove backgrounds in your browser. Export individual pages or the full carousel, or attach them to your post.
  • Video editor. Trim and arrange clips on a multitrack timeline. Add captions, transitions, effects, and audio. Cut footage by selecting words in its transcript.
  • Recorder. Capture your screen, camera, and microphone, then bring the recording into your video edit.
  • Calendar and queues. Choose a publishing time or the next free slot in a queue. Plan your posts in the calendar, stagger publishing across accounts, and get reminders when your queue runs low.
  • Auto repost. Set delays and engagement rules for native reposts on supported networks. Override the defaults for individual posts.
  • Media library. Keep reusable images, videos, fonts, and brand assets in your workspace. Organize files with tags, collections, and favorites. Attach saved media without uploading it again.
  • Meme maker. Pick a template, edit its captions, and replace its images. Save the result to your media library or attach it to a post.
  • Inbox and messages. Read and reply to supported comments and direct messages without switching between social apps.
  • Analytics and repurposing. Compare post results and track audience changes across your accounts. Use a previous post as the starting point for a new draft.
  • Find people. Find accounts to follow on Bluesky and Mastodon. Review the suggestions and choose who to follow.
  • AI writing and ideas. Generate ideas, compare draft directions, and adapt posts for your selected platforms. You review the result before publishing. AI writing is optional. Self-hosted AI setup.
  • API, SDK, CLI, MCP, and n8n. Create drafts, schedule posts, and check publishing results from your scripts, agents, or n8n workflows. Install the TypeScript SDK or the CLI from npm. See the API reference for endpoints.
  • Teams and workspaces. Keep brands and clients in separate workspaces and control who can edit them. Protect your account with passkeys, two-factor authentication, and session controls.

Supported platforms

OpenPost has integrations for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pixelfed, PeerTube, Lemmy, PieFed, Discord, Pinterest, and Telegram. Mastodon and Pixelfed connect per instance over OAuth; PeerTube, Lemmy, and PieFed connect with instance credentials. Discord supports incoming webhooks and a bot integration; Telegram uses a bot.

Posting formats, inbox features, and analytics vary by platform. Some integrations require provider app approval or publicly reachable media URLs.
